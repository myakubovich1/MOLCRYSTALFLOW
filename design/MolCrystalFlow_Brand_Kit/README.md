# MolCrystalFlow Brand Kit

## Core idea
MolCrystalFlow is positioned as advanced computational materials infrastructure: precise, fast, technical, and future-facing.

## Logo system
- Primary lockup: metallic MCF monogram with diagonal flow line and full wordmark.
- Icon-only: use for app icons, avatars, favicons, and compact navigation.
- Wordmark-only: use where the symbol already appears nearby.
- White and black one-color variants are included for simple production uses.

## Clear space
Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the central crystal facet. Do not place text, borders, or other logos inside this zone.

## Minimum sizes
- Full lockup: 180 px digital / 35 mm print.
- Icon: 24 px digital / 8 mm print.
- Wordmark: 140 px digital / 28 mm print.

## Colors
- Void Black: #050709
- Graphite: #11161C
- Metal Silver: #C8CDD4
- Ice White: #F5F7FA
- Ion Blue: #1A83FF

Use Ion Blue sparingly as a signal color, not as a dominant fill.

## Typography
Recommended:
- Display / headings: Space Grotesk, Medium or SemiBold
- UI / body: Inter, Regular or Medium
- Code / scientific data: IBM Plex Mono

The logo wordmark itself should remain artwork and should not be retyped.

## Voice
Precise. Direct. Technical. Calm. Avoid exaggerated claims, playful science clichés, or overly promotional language.
